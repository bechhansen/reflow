# Iron Stand

Cradle that holds the clothes iron inverted (soleplate facing up) as the reflow hotplate.

## Purpose
Stable platform that keeps the iron securely upside-down during operation. The soleplate becomes the working surface for PCBs.

## Print Settings
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Material  | ABS or ASA (must tolerate radiated heat from the soleplate) |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Infill | 40% — must be structurally solid |
| Supports | Yes — for the iron cradle overhangs |
| Wall count | 4 minimum |

## Design Notes
- Measure the body of your iron before modelling — shape varies between brands
- Feet should be rubber-tipped or have rubber inserts to prevent sliding
- Leave clearance around the power cord exit of the iron
- The stand should raise the soleplate at least 30 mm off the work surface to allow airflow underneath

## Safety
- Never leave the iron unattended during operation
- Ensure the stand cannot tip over — the centre of gravity of an inverted iron is high
- Keep flammable materials away from the soleplate work area
